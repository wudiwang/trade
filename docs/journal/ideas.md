# Ideas

## AI Watch Agent

Use AI to monitor selected symbols on 1m charts, summarize changes, detect symbolic K lines, alert when a setup becomes actionable, and abandon setups that lose value.

## Strategy Lab

Make local backtesting more than a signal list. It should preserve original ideas, classic charts, metrics, changelogs, and implementation semantics.

## Macro Regime

Track BTC regime and macro backdrop. Include analyst video summaries and liquidity conditions as context for altcoin risk.

## Strong Mover Top · Weak Rebound Continuation

Archive strong coins whose final acceleration is followed by a sharp drop, a 5m rebound that cannot recover the reference midpoint, prolonged low consolidation, and a second breakdown. First case: `research/ideas/007-strong-mover-top-weak-rebound/`.
